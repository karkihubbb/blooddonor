How to use the Biratnagar donor directory

  1. Open the donor search and choose the blood group you are looking for: A+, A−, B+, B−, AB+, AB−, O+ or O−.
  2. Enter Biratnagar, a ward number or a nearby area to narrow the public listings.
  3. Review the listed area and use the contact request on a donor card to start the contact process.
  4. Confirm the donor's current availability and blood compatibility with a licensed blood bank or hospital before making arrangements.

Areas covered by the registration form

The donor registration form includes Biratnagar wards 1–19, Itahari, Rangeli, other areas of Morang and an option for another location. These are location choices for volunteers; they are not a promise that a donor is currently available in every area.

What the listings mean

Public listings are based on volunteers who have agreed to be included. The directory is a community contact service, not a blood bank, hospital, emergency service or medical authority. A listing does not guarantee that a volunteer is available for a particular request.

Important: For urgent medical needs, contact a hospital or licensed blood bank directly. A health professional or blood bank must confirm donor eligibility and blood compatibility.

Privacy and donor consent

People registering through the website are asked to agree to public listing of their name, blood group, area and contact details. Only submit your own details, and register only when you understand and agree to the form's consent statement. Donor phone numbers are not printed as plain text on the public donor cards; use the website's contact-request feature.
