Looking for blood in Biratnagar?

Use BiratnagarBloodNetwork to search public listings from voluntary donors who have agreed to be contacted. Filter by blood group and area, then confirm the details directly with a hospital or licensed blood bank.

Search local donor listingsHow the donor directory works

Steps to search for a donor

  1. Contact the hospital or licensed blood bank caring for the patient and confirm the required blood group and next steps.
  2. Open the BiratnagarBloodNetwork donor directory.
  3. Select the required blood group and enter Biratnagar, a ward or another nearby area.
  4. Use the contact-request option on a public donor card, then confirm whether the volunteer is currently available.
  5. Let the hospital or blood bank complete all required medical checks and compatibility testing.

Search by blood group

The directory supports A+, A−, B+, B−, AB+, AB−, O+ and O−. Select the group specified by the treating hospital or blood bank. Do not rely on a website listing or blood-group label to decide whether a donation is medically suitable.

Search by area

The registration form supports Biratnagar wards 1–19, Itahari, Rangeli, other areas of Morang and an additional-location option. Donor listings depend on volunteers who choose to register and agree to public listing. The site does not guarantee coverage or immediate availability in every area.

For urgent or emergency needs: contact the treating hospital or a licensed blood bank directly. BiratnagarBloodNetwork is a community directory, not an emergency-response service or a source of medical advice.

Can the website guarantee a donor?

No. The website helps people discover consenting public donor listings and request contact. A listed person may not be available when contacted. Availability, donor eligibility and blood compatibility must be confirmed by qualified hospital or blood-bank staff.

Protect donor privacy

Use the site's contact-request feature rather than copying or sharing another person's details. Do not submit someone else's information to the directory without their permission.
